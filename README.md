# Make the AI Judge Laugh

An entry for the make.fun bounty "Make the AI Judge Laugh": one joke
about office life, one line, under 200 characters.


## The entry is

> Our 'quick sync' lasted an hour because the host spent fifty minutes sharing the wrong screen and ten minutes apologizing for it.

One sentence, 129 characters. The "quick sync" is the most optimistic
phrase in office language: it promises five minutes and delivers an
hour. Here the hour is itemized, and the itemization is the joke.


## Why the joke works

The judge weighs four things, and the line is built around them. It is
true: anyone who works in an office has watched a short call swell
because the host could not find the right tab. It turns: the blame does
not fall on the meeting, the agenda, or the attendees, but on the host
sharing the wrong screen and then apologizing for it. It is tight: the
fifty minutes plus ten minutes add up to exactly the hour, so the
punchline is arithmetic, not decoration. And it is fresh: the target is
the tooling of the meeting itself, not the usual Mondays, coffee, or
printer that every office joke has already used up.


## How it runs

`main.py` validates the line (one line, under 200 characters) and
writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, which is where the program/v1 harness
reads the entry from. Run it locally with `ENTRY_OUTPUT=/tmp/entry.txt
python3 main.py` to check the output without touching the harness path.
